## A laser platform for demanding rooms

The DX Series is built around a 0.65-inch single-chip DLP optical engine and a laser light source. Across the series, brightness ranges from 5,000 to 6,500 lm with 1920 × 1080 resolution and a choice of 16:10 or 16:9 aspect ratio.

It is intended for large meeting rooms, school lecture theatres and stage environments where a clear, consistent image is part of the room's daily work.

## Three models

The series includes the DX-50, DX-60 and DX-70. Fixed and motorized lens options are available, allowing the projector to be matched to the installation.

Model-specific configurations are available on request.

## Built for installation

A 0.65-inch DLP optical engine and laser light source sit inside a housing designed for professional installation. For project planning, contact the laser projection division with the room dimensions, intended screen size and application.
